# Content and illustration pass

Completed September 21, 2026. Copy review and selected source checks took place September 18.

The pass covers exactly the existing 54 selected posts: six in each of nine categories. It preserves original publication dates, URLs, source links and the supplied design. The separate original backup and its 32 archived articles were not edited.

## Content

All 54 posts received a reader-focused edit and a topic-specific practical explanation or reading exercise. Process commentary was removed from the main narrative where it distracted from the subject. Selected headlines and descriptions were clarified. Historical claims remain dated and attributed; proposals, announced plans, legal allegations and confirmed outcomes remain distinct.

The V&A exhibition closing date was corrected to November 8, 2026, using the museum listing checked September 18. The WHO cruise-ship post now states the report's moderate risk classification for passengers and crew alongside low global risk. The SoftBank post distinguishes its three planned investment tranches from completed payments.

Selected official sources were rechecked, as listed below. This pass does not claim that every source link or every subsequent development was freshly verified. Each post retains its earlier source-revision note and has a separate content/illustration update note.

## Artwork

There are 54 distinct, original editorial illustrations, one per selected post. They were generated with the built-in image_gen tool in a consistent gouache-and-ink style and inspected visually. The 1536 × 1024 PNG files are supplied unchanged from generation. They are conceptual images, not photographs, depictions of documented scenes or reconstructions of named people's appearances.

Post heroes, home/category previews, search cards and sharing metadata use the matching artwork. Captions identify AI-generated illustrations, and alternative text describes the subject. The original logo and interface graphics remain. Large PNG originals are included for editing; cards use native browser lazy loading. An optional future hosting optimization can create smaller delivery variants from these masters.

The exact prompts, public asset paths and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in Illustration-prompts.json. Editable masters are also in source-code/illustrations/ inside the ZIP; deployable copies are in site/img/illustrations/.

The follow-up placeholder pass also fills every banner, newsletter and former advertising image slot with illustrations from this set, including both light and dark theme versions. Existing slot dimensions, theme styles, the site logo and interface icons are preserved. Former ad slots now link to the illustrated blog post and are labeled accordingly. The placeholder totals are recorded in validation.json.
