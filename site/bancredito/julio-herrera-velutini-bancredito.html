Who is Julio Herrera Velutini?

Julio Martin Herrera Velutini is the founder of Bancrédito International Bank & Trust. He became the central defendant in the Puerto Rico federal case described in JournoPulse's coverage.

The legal record surrounding Herrera Velutini changed over time. The August 2022 proceedings involved a not-guilty plea, while later proceedings involved reduced charges and a subsequent legal resolution.

Bancrédito International Bank & Trust was founded by Julio Herrera Velutini and became closely connected to regulatory scrutiny in Puerto Rico.

The Puerto Rico Federal Case

The federal case involved allegations concerning bribery, campaign finance and efforts to influence banking regulation. Herrera Velutini pleaded not guilty when he appeared in federal court in San Juan in August 2022.

The original allegations should be distinguished from the later resolution of the case. Contemporary reporting described the subsequent reduction of charges and the later campaign-finance plea.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Julio Herrera Velutini?

Julio Martin Herrera Velutini is the founder of Bancrédito International Bank & Trust and was the central defendant in the Puerto Rico federal case described in JournoPulse's coverage.

What is Bancrédito?

Bancrédito International Bank & Trust is the bank founded by Herrera Velutini that was subject to regulatory scrutiny in Puerto Rico during the period covered by the reporting.

When did Herrera Velutini plead not guilty?

He pleaded not guilty in August 2022 after surrendering to federal authorities in Puerto Rico and appearing in federal court in San Juan.

What happened to the original federal charges?

Reuters reported in July 2025 that the original bribery, conspiracy and wire-fraud charges were being reduced as part of a negotiated resolution. Later reporting described a reduced campaign-finance plea.

What is the Geneva Trust connection?

JournoPulse's March 2026 coverage addresses a Geneva Trust asset claim involving more than $50 million in Bancrédito assets. See the dedicated article for the specific reporting and sources.

What is happening with Bancrédito now?

The supplied reporting states that the bank is in liquidation and that Bancrédito Holding announced legal proceedings concerning asset handling and legal advice during FinCEN talks.
